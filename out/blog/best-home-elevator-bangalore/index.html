Best Home Elevators in Bangalore: Complete Guide for Villas & Duplexes

Bangalore's residential landscape has shifted rapidly toward multi-storey independent homes, luxury villas in corridors like Whitefield, Sarjapur, and Hebbal, and spacious duplex apartments in Koramangala and Jayanagar. Installing a residential lift is no longer just a luxury upgrade—it is a practical investment in family accessibility, senior comfort, and long-term property value.

This guide breaks down the best home elevator options available in Bengaluru, civil space requirements, technology platforms, and why Teknix Elevators leads the home mobility market.

Why Bangalore Homeowners Are Installing Home Lifts

  1. Elderly Accessibility: Eliminates stair-climbing hurdles for aging parents, allowing comfortable mobility across all floors.
  2. Compact Footprint Integration: Modern home lifts require minimal space and can often fit inside stairwell cutouts or exterior glass shafts.
  3. Property Value Enhancement: Installing a home lift adds significant resale value and future-proofs independent homes in Bengaluru's competitive real estate market.
  4. Energy Efficiency: Advanced residential drives operate on domestic single-phase power connections (220V) with minimal electricity consumption.

Standard Technical Specifications for Home Lifts

A standard 2 to 4-passenger home lift (272 kg to 400 kg capacity) provides an optimal balance of standing space and compact shaft requirements:

  • Cabin Internal Size: 1,000 mm (W) x 900 mm (D) x 2,200 mm (H)
  • Civil Shaft Size Needed: ~1,400 mm (W) x 1,300 mm (D)
  • Pit Depth: 300 mm to 1,100 mm (low-pit options available for retrofits)
  • Overhead Clearance: 3,200 mm to 3,800 mm
  • Doors: Automatic telescopic doors or space-saving manual swing glass doors
