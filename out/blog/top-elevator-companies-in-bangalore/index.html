Top Elevator Companies in Bangalore: Complete Guide for Homes & Businesses

Bangalore's rapid urban growth—from high-rise IT parks in Electronic City and Outer Ring Road to luxury duplexes in Indiranagar and Sadashivanagar—has created an unprecedented demand for reliable vertical transportation. Choosing the right elevator partner is critical to ensuring safety, long-term durability, smooth maintenance (AMC), and strict compliance with Indian Standards (IS 14665).

This guide outlines the top elevator companies operating in Bengaluru, comparing global giants, domestic leaders, and local specialists.

1. Teknix Elevators

Headquartered in Bengaluru, Teknix Elevators has established itself as a premier elevator manufacturer offering German-engineered, custom-designed vertical mobility solutions. Teknix specializes in Machine Room-Less (MRL) technology, luxury residential home lifts, high-speed commercial elevators, and heavy-duty goods lifts.

  • Key Strengths: German precision components, customizable Italian cabin interiors, low-energy single-phase solutions, and direct local after-sales service across Bengaluru.
  • Best For: Luxury independent villas, duplex homes, residential apartments, boutique offices, and commercial buildings.
  • Head Office: V V Puram (Near Lalbagh West Gate), Bengaluru - 560004.

2. KONE India

KONE is a global leader in elevator and escalator technology with a heavy operational presence in Bengaluru. Renowned for its EcoDisc technology and regenerative drives, KONE is a primary choice for large-scale real estate and commercial projects.

  • Key Strengths: High energy efficiency, advanced destination control systems, and broad maintenance infrastructure.
  • Best For: High-rise residential towers, IT tech parks, commercial malls, and multi-specialty hospitals.

3. Otis India

As one of the world's oldest and most recognizable elevator brands, Otis maintains a strong local manufacturing and service footprint near Bengaluru (Jigani Industrial Area).

  • Key Strengths: Deep engineering heritage, heavy load capacity, and robust safety protocols.
  • Best For: Mid-to-high-rise residential developments, commercial towers, and transit hubs.

4. Schindler India

Schindler offers Swiss-engineered elevator and escalator solutions with a strong sales and technical service hub located in J.P. Nagar, Bengaluru.

  • Key Strengths: Modern aesthetic options, digital transit management, and eco-friendly drive systems.
  • Best For: Corporate headquarters, luxury residential complexes, and modernization of older buildings.

5. Johnson Lifts

Johnson Lifts is one of India's largest domestic elevator brands, maintaining a strong regional service center in Rajajinagar, Bengaluru.

  • Key Strengths: Widespread service reach across Karnataka, cost-effective pricing, and reliable local supply chains.
  • Best For: Budget-conscious residential apartments, public infrastructure, and commercial spaces.

Key Factors to Evaluate Before Choosing an Elevator Supplier

  1. Space & Shaft Efficiency: Ask whether the manufacturer requires a dedicated machine room or offers Machine Room-Less (MRL) equipment.
  2. Power Requirements: Check if the lift can run on single-phase domestic power (ideal for home lifts) or requires a dedicated three-phase connection.
  3. Safety Gear & Backup: Ensure standard inclusion of Automatic Rescue Device (ARD), emergency alarm, overload sensors, infrared door curtains, and manual lower mechanics.
  4. Local AMC Service Support: Verify that the company maintains a dedicated 24/7 service station and local spare parts warehouse in Bengaluru.
